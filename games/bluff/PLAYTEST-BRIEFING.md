# BLUFF first-play check (AVR-90, for AVR-27)

The question this answers: can four people who have never played BLUFF reach normal play, and
play a first round, without anyone teaching them the rules? Only real people can answer it.
Nothing here has been run yet.

## Setup

- Four phones on the Avrana Party Wi-Fi. Each phone's browser has never acknowledged the
  briefing: use a fresh browser profile or private tab, or clear the site's storage.
- The owner **does not explain the rules**. The owner may only say: "Join the party, then play
  BLUFF." If a player asks a rules question, the owner answers "Is it in the ? button?" and notes
  the question.
- One observer, with a timer and this sheet.

## Run

1. Everyone joins, and the game opens.
2. Time each player from when the briefing appears to when they press **I'm ready**.
3. Play one full game. Note every moment someone hesitates, asks a question or does something by
   mistake.
4. Afterwards, ask each player the three questions below.

**After the game, ask each player:**
1. What is the goal?
2. What happens if you challenge someone who was telling the truth?
3. What can stop a Steal?

Record the answers word for word, and mark each one right or wrong.

## Pass / fail

- **Pass:**
  - all four reach normal play without the owner explaining a rule;
  - the median time to "I'm ready" is at most about 90 s;
  - at least three of the four answer all three questions correctly.
- **Evidence for the shared onboarding contract:** every question and mistake, whether or not the
  check passed.
